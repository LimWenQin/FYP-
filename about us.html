About Our Donation Centre

Who We Are

We are a non-profit organization that manages donations for various causes including elderly homes, orphanages, and facilities for people with disabilities. Our mission is to build a convenient and transparent online donation platform for the public to help those in need.

Our Vision

To create a society where generosity and compassion become accessible through technology, making it easier for people to give and make a difference.

Our Goals

We aim to provide a trusted platform where donors can easily make one-time or monthly donations, view fundraising progress, and track how their contributions are making an impact.
